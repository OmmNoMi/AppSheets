Executive Summary
OmmNoMi Automation LLP has successfully engineered and deployed two major operational enhancements to the BLR World Orbit HRMS platform: (1) Unified Employee Identification Labeling on the LeaveAllocation table, concatenating employee code and full legal name for instant visual auditing; and (2) Approved Leave Cascading Deletion & Balance Restoration Engine on AttendanceRequest. Diplomatic Context: Extending Platform Capabilities Beyond Standard SOP
Under standard ledger accounting procedures (SOP), historical approved leave transactions are strictly immutable to prevent balance skew and data fragmentation. Consequently, deleting approved past leave records was deliberately excluded from the baseline engineering scope in June 2026 to guarantee ledger safety.

However, acknowledging BLR World HR's ongoing operational requirement to rectify retrospective submission mistakes without forcing manual credit adjustments, OmmNoMi has engineered an intelligent multi-tiered cascading solution. Rather than merely unblocking the delete button (which would cause data corruption), we architected a synchronous orchestration chain that purges child calendar records and immediately restores employee leave quotas in a single transaction.
Feature 1: Unified Employee Labeling (`LeaveAllocation`)

Previously, allocation sheets and balance selector cards displayed raw system IDs or isolated names, creating ambiguity during batch audits for staff sharing common names. We injected a persistent Virtual Column combining employee code with full names.

Component Location Formula / Definition Operational Benefit
EmployeeLabel Virtual Column on LeaveAllocation CONCATENATE([Employee].[Code], " - ", [Employee].[FirstName], " ", [Employee].[LastName]) Zero-ambiguity visual lookup across dropdowns, cards, and audits.
Feature 2: Cascading Deletion & Balance Resync Engine

To avoid asynchronous bot latency and eliminate the AppSheet [_THISROW_BEFORE] expression syntax limitation, the engine runs client-side as a 4-tier coordinated execution chain:

Execution Step Action / Target Technical Formula & Logic Safety Outcome
1. Child Purge Act_Delete_Daily_Child on AttendanceDaily FILTER("AttendanceDaily", AND([AttendanceRequest] = [_THISROW].[ID], IN([Status], {"Att_PL", "Att_UL", "Att_WFH", "Att_R"}))) Purges all daily entries generated by the request. Zero orphaned logs.
2. Balance Restitution Sync this LeaveAllocation Action - 1 Executes on LeaveAllocation for the targeted employee and leave type. Recalculates Used Days reactive total; restores available quota instantly.
3. Sibling Resync Sync this AttendanceRequest Action - 1 Executes across sibling requests for employee calendar alignment. Synchronizes request status across connected views.
4. Parent Removal System Delete on AttendanceRequest Standard AppSheet Row Deletion (triggered as final step of group). Permanent removal of the request header after full cleanup.

Strict Protection for Biometric & Geofenced Records
Time Off in Lieu (TOIL) and Attendance Regularization requests remain strictly non-deletable: NOT(IN([RequestType], {"Time Off in Lieu (TOIL)", "Attendance Regularization"})). Because TOIL and Regularizations adjust statutory biometric punch clocks and geofenced timesheets, their audit trail is legally protected and must remain permanently preserved.

Operational FAQ for Orbit HR Teams
What happens if an employee submits a leave by mistake and it gets approved?
An authorized People Admin or SuperAdmin can open the request and tap Delete. All booked calendar days will vanish, and the employee's leave balance will be restored automatically.
Why can't managers delete Regularization or TOIL requests?
Regularizations and TOIL affect legal biometric attendance punches and overtime tracking. To guarantee payroll integrity and audit compliance, these cannot be deleted.
