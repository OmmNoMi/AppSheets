1. Summary of Mismatch

An audit has been conducted regarding the false-positive alert triggered by the Orbit check-in reminder bot. Although the employee's mobile interface confirmed that she checked in at 8:19 AM local time, an automated reminder was dispatched to her at 9:30 AM GST (8:30 AM Lebanon time) stating she had not checked in yet.

The audit of cell history and background sync logs indicates that the check-in occurred on time locally but was stored in the device's local queue. The data did not synchronize to the AppSheet cloud database until 10:36 AM local time, resulting in a 2-hour, 17-minute synchronization lag.
