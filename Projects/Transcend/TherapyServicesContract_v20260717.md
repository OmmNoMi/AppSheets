Welcome to Transcend Counseling & Wellness. Here at Transcend, we love what we do and are glad you are here! This document contains important information about our professional services and business policies that will allow us and our clients to be successful together. Please read it carefully and make note of any questions you may have so that you can discuss them with your provider during your first meeting. Once signed, this document will constitute a binding agreement between us.

By the end of your first or second meeting, you and your therapist will have agreed on one or more outcomes for your work together and, if you decide to continue, the first steps in the plan to achieve those outcomes. This plan will include therapy and, if you desire, may include other wellness services which will be explained to you in detail at the time those services are recommended. Your therapist will provide you with suggestions about which services may be most helpful to you, but you are free to select the services you wish to receive. You should evaluate the information provided to you along with your own assessment about whether your therapist is someone with whom you would feel comfortable working. Therapy involves a large commitment of time, money, and energy, so you should be very careful about the therapist you select. If you have questions, please discuss them with your therapist whenever they arise. If you have doubts that persist, we will be happy to help you secure an appropriate consultation with another mental health professional either in our practice or with a different practice, again depending on your preference.

We believe that integrating spirituality into our therapy and therapy practices is an essential factor in providing care for the whole person - mind, body, and spirit. 

## THERAPY SERVICES

Therapy is not easily described in general statements. It varies depending on the personality of both the counselor and the client and the particular concerns the client wishes to address. It is not like visiting a medical doctor because it requires a very active role on your part. In order to be most successful, you will have to work hard both during and between sessions.

Therapy has both benefits and risks. Risks sometimes include experiencing uncomfortable feelings like sadness, guilt, anxiety, anger, frustration, loneliness, and helplessness. Therapy may include remembering uncomfortable experiences or doing things in a new way, both of which can be challenging. Therapy has also been demonstrated to have benefits for those who undertake it. It often leads to a significant reduction of feelings of distress, better relationships, and resolution of specific problems. There are, however, no guarantees about what will happen.

## MEETINGS

The frequency of your therapy sessions depends on the concerns you are addressing, the outcomes you desire and the type of therapy we are providing to you. Therapy sessions are generally scheduled one to two times per week at the beginning and then spaced out as therapy continues and progress is made. It may be possible to accomplish the outcomes you desire more quickly by meeting more frequently. Please feel free to discuss with your therapist the possibility of meeting more frequently if this is a concern for you. 

The length of each session depends on the type of service provided, but will rarely exceed 60 minutes. The minimum durations are listed below:

| Type of Service | Duration |
| :--- | :--- |
| Initial Consultation and Evaluation | 40 minutes |
| Therapy Session – Private Pay | 45 minutes |
| Therapy Session – Insurance | 38 minutes |
| Therapy Session, multiple interventions – Insurance | 53 minutes |

If you are using your insurance benefits, and you arrive late to your session or you elect to discontinue the session prior to the minimum duration, you will be responsible to pay the difference between what the insurance plan covers for the full-session and a partial-session. 

## PROFESSIONAL RECORDS

Both the law and the standards of the counseling profession require that we keep treatment records. These records always include your contact information, dates and duration of appointments, receipts of payment, treatment objectives, and progress being made. You are entitled to receive a copy of the records we keep, and if you wish, your therapist can prepare an appropriate summary for you. Clients will be charged an appropriate fee for any preparation time or material that is required to comply with an information request.

## BILLING AND PAYMENTS

You will be expected to pay for each session the day the service is rendered, unless prior arrangements are made. Payment schedules for professional services other than psychotherapy will be agreed to at the time these services are requested. In circumstances of unusual financial hardship, we may be willing to negotiate an installment payment plan.

### INVOICES
- An invoice is created the morning of the appointment
- The invoice is for the contracted rate showing the total amount due, including:
  - Your portion, if any
  - The insurance company portion, if any*
- *The invoice will continue to show unpaid until the insurance company has delivered reimbursement

### PAYMENT METHOD
- It is our policy to require a payment method be kept on file for any out-of-pocket fees for the services we provide.
- Payment options include Visa or Mastercard
- Credit or debit cards, including HSA and FSA, are accepted
- We cannot accept American Express or Discover cards
- The payment information is stored in your account in a manner that cannot be accessed by staff other than the practice owner and the head of the billing department.
- The payment method is charged the morning of the service.
- Our therapists do not take payments
- A receipt of the charge will be emailed to you, unless you opt out of email communication.
- You may request a statement of your account at any time.

## OTHER SERVICES

In addition to therapy sessions, it is our practice to charge our standard rate on a prorated basis, with a minimum of $50.00, for other professional services you may require such as report writing, document preparation, record preparation, treatment summaries, telephone conversations lasting longer than 10 minutes, attendance at meetings or consultations with other professionals you have authorized. For legal proceedings, we charge $250.00 per hour.

**MEDICAL RECORD REQUESTS:** $30.00 per hour on a prorated basis, with a minimum of $30.00, for any medical record request not related to the continuation of care. Allow 30 days for preparation.

**CANCELLATIONS:** Minimum of 48 business hours (2 business days) notice required for re-scheduling or canceling an appointment. Appointments canceled or changed without sufficient notice will be subject to a missed appointment fee equal to the entire contracted rate.

## CONFIDENTIALITY & TREATMENT OF MINORS

Confidentiality of all communications between a client and a mental health professional is protected by law. Exceptions include: risk of harm to self/others, child/elder abuse, court orders. For minors under 18, standards require consent from both parents prior to engaging in therapy.
