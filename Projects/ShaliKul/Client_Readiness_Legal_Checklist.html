Executive Directive for the Brand Owner
Under the Asset-Light Third-Party Manufacturing Model, you do not require factory permits, environmental clearances, or blending plant licenses—those statutory liabilities rest 100% with the contract bottler. However, Indian law holds you as the Brand Owner & Marketer strictly accountable for Entity Registration, Brand Ownership (Trademarks), Packaging Metrology (Rule 27), and Truth-in-Advertising. This checklist identifies what you must execute, what is already done, and the key strategic decisions needed from you.

Phase C: Legal Metrology Rule 27 & Packaging Mandates

Phase D: Certification Decisions & Legally Safe Marketing Claims
⚠️ Consumer Protection Act & BIS Compliance Warning
Never claim official IS 5759 Type II Certified or display the BIS ISI logo unless your contract plant holds an active BIS license for your brand. Printing unauthorized government marks risks immediate seizure under Section 11 of the BIS Act, 2016.
Certification Option Pros & Strategic Commercial Value Cons & Operational Friction Client Decision Recommendation
Voluntary BIS License (IS 5759 ISI Mark) Unlocks state government transport fleet tenders (HRTC, UPSRTC) and institutional bus contracts. High annual license fees, mandatory factory audits, and 3-6 month lead time. Not needed for garage retail.
