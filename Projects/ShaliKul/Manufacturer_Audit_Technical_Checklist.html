Quality & Legal Directive for Manufacturing Partners
To qualify as an approved contract manufacturing partner for SHALI KUL™, the plant must verify full compliance with all statutory environmental consents, chemical formulation standards, in-house laboratory testing, and automated induction sealing. Completion of this audit and delivery of verified document copies is a strict pre-condition prior to the issuance of Purchase Orders or packaging dispatches.
