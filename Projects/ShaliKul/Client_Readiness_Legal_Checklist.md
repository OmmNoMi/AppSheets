## 1. Executive Purpose

This checklist serves as the **operational master roadmap for the Brand Owner (Client)**. Because SHALI KUL™ operates under an **Asset-Light Third-Party Manufacturing Model**, you do not need chemical plant licenses or heavy machinery permits. 

However, as the **Brand Owner & Marketer**, Indian law places strict statutory responsibility on you regarding **Entity Registration, Brand Ownership (IP), Consumer Protection, and Legal Metrology (Packaging)**.

Use this checklist to track what is **Done**, what is **In Progress**, and what **Decisions** you need to make before launching.

### Phase D: Strategic Decisions on Certifications & Claims

> [!WARNING]
> Under the **Consumer Protection Act, 2019** and the **Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016**, printing unauthorized government marks (such as BIS ISI mark or NABL accreditation logos) on product labels without a valid factory license carries severe statutory penalties and product seizure.

| # | Certification Decision | Pros & Cons Analysis | Client Decision Needed | Recommendation |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D1** | **Voluntary BIS Certification (IS 5759)** | • **Pros:** Official ISI mark builds instant trust for state government fleet tenders.<br>• **Cons:** Long audit process (3-6 months), requires manufacturer plant inspection and high annual royalty fees. | `[ ] Apply for BIS`<br>`[ ] Defer to Phase 2`<br>`[ ] Not Required` | **DEFER TO PHASE 2**<br>*(Focus on independent garage retail first).* |
| **D2** | **NABL Accredited Third-Party Lab Test Report** | • **Pros:** Cost-effective (~₹8,000–₹15,000 per sample). Provides a verifiable independent test certificate showing boiling point >155°C, freezing point, and anti-corrosion metrics without needing ongoing ISI audits.<br>• **Cons:** Valid for that specific tested batch only. | `[ ] Commission Test`<br>`[ ] Rely on Mfg COA`<br>`[ ] Pending` | **STRONGLY RECOMMENDED**<br>*(Keep 1 NABL report in sales kit for dealers).* |
| **D3** | **ISO 9001:2015 Certification (Brand Trading)** | • **Pros:** Enhances corporate marketing prestige on letterheads and marketing materials.<br>• **Cons:** Purely operational quality management, does not test the chemical product itself. | `[ ] Obtain ISO`<br>`[ ] Defer`<br>`[ ] Not Required` | **OPTIONAL (Phase 2)** |
| **D4** | **Packaging Claims Language Policy** | • Legally safe: *"Formulated to meet Japanese JIS K 2234 & Indian IS 5759 performance benchmarks"*, *"100% Quality Guaranteed"*, *"Multi-Metal Safe"*, *"Anti-Boil & Anti-Rust Protection"*.<br>• Avoid: Claiming direct OEM approval or official BIS licensing unless certificates exist. | `[ ] Copy Approved`<br>`[ ] In Review`<br>`[ ] Pending` | **MANDATORY POLICY** |
